# Code of Conduct

admino is a small project, and I'd like it to stay a welcoming one. This applies to
everyone taking part — in issues, pull requests, and any other project space.

## The short version

Be respectful and assume good faith. Argue about code, not about people. Give
newcomers room to learn.

## Not welcome

Harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind. Unwanted personal attention.
Publishing someone's private information without their consent. Persistent bad-faith
disruption. In short: anything that makes others feel unsafe or unwelcome.

## Reporting a problem

If you experience or witness any of this, report it privately through the
repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** form — it reaches the maintainer
directly and stays confidential.

The maintainer may edit or remove offending content and, for serious or repeated
behaviour, block people from the project. I'll aim to be fair and use good judgement.
